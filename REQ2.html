Team 7 CS250 Project

Every year in San Diego county, wildfires kill dozens of people, displace hundreds of thousands, and destroy thousands of homes. Knowing when and where a wildire is likely to start is an invaluable tool in reducing the harm done by them. This website has been made to help with just that. Simply input temperature, days since rain, humidity, and wind speed in your area, and this website will calculate the risk of a wildfire starting on that day, and output it in the specified field. Make sure to input your values in the metric system.
